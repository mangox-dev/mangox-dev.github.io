Sign-in and account

For a problem with LINE Login or Sign in with Apple, tell us which one you use, your device, and what the app showed. Include your MangoX display name if you know it.

Passes and payment errors

If you paid and your pass did not activate: on iPhone and iPad, open the My tab and tap “Restore purchases”; on Android, reopen the app while signed in to the same account. If that does not solve it, email us with the product name, the purchase date, and the order number from your Apple or Google receipt.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, and refund requests follow their procedures:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play's refund procedure

See also our Refund & Cancellation Policy (Korean).

Privacy

For questions or requests about your data, read our Privacy Policy and email support@mangox.app.

Account deletion

When you are signed in, tap the account icon at the top right of the screen and select “Delete account”. Your request is recorded first and processed after verification; deletion is not immediate. If you cannot use the app, follow the account deletion instructions.

Safety

To report abusive behavior or a safety issue, use the report function in the app or email us. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. See our Child Safety Standards.
