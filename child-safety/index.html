MangoX · Legal

Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 4 July 2026

This English version is the primary version. Translations are provided for user convenience.

MangoX is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

MangoX prohibits any content, behavior, or activity involving child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexualization of minors, solicitation, trafficking, exploitation, or any attempt to share, request, or promote abusive content involving minors.

Users can report child safety concerns, suspected CSAE, or other safety issues through the in-app support/report flow or by contacting support@mangox.app.

MangoX reviews reported safety concerns and may restrict, suspend, or remove accounts that violate these standards.

Where required by applicable law, MangoX will report illegal child safety content or conduct to the appropriate authorities or designated reporting organizations.

MangoX is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy.

For operator or company contact, users may also contact netai@netai.co.kr where applicable.
