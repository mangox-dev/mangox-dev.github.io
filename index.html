How MangoX works

Three steps inside the venue.

A simple loop built for a real night out — check in on location, show interest in people you like, and connect only when interest is mutual.

STEP 01
📍

Check In

Arrive at a participating venue and check in on location to enter the night's session.

STEP 02
⭐

Connect

Send Hearts, Star and Pick3 to people you're interested in. They are private signals between the two of you.

STEP 03
🔗

Connect

A connection becomes available only after mutual interest — never before.

No in-app chat.
Connections are available only after mutual interest.
